THE FIRST BOTTLENECK

Your Bakery fills in about 3h 33m.

At 1 Bread every 80 seconds and a 160-Bread cap, production reaches the limit surprisingly quickly. If nobody collects it, the next hours cannot keep stacking into the Bakery.

FULL

Blackfire AutopilotCollect before the cap

Automatic collection keeps reopening capacity throughout the day instead of waiting for your next manual check-in.
